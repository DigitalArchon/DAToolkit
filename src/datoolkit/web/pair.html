Step 1 of 2: check the certificate

Before connecting, make sure this phone is talking to your DA Toolkit and not to something in between. Compare the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint with the one shown on the DA Toolkit screen.

Compare with the DA Toolkit screen, never with anything on this page. This page can't prove where it came from; only the certificate can.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the icon at the left of the address bar ("Not secure").
  2. Tap Certificate is not valid (or Connection is not secure), then Certificate information / Certificate details.
  3. Find the SHA-256 fingerprint.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. On the "This connection is not private" warning, tap Show Details, then view the certificate.
  2. Tap More Details and scroll to Fingerprints → SHA-256.

Then

  1. Every pair matches: tap Fingerprint matches in DA Toolkit and scan the second code.
  2. Anything differs: stop. Don't scan the second code. Someone may be intercepting traffic on this network.

After scanning the second code, the browser must not warn about the certificate again. If it does, stop and pair again from step 1.
